- land · Layered Landscape: 3–5 receding terrain layers (hills, forest, mountains, dunes, fields, city blocks), with app-specific details on the near layer and haze between layers.
  - Loop: parallax drift while the light warms and cools.
  - Pitfall: busy near-layer details.
- window · Window View: a strong frame centered behind the name (an airplane window, porthole, room window, windshield, viewfinder: whatever the app's user would look through), with the outside behind it.
  - Loop: the outside changes.
  - Pitfall: the frame crowding the name.
- horizon · Horizon: a water line or skyline at about two-thirds of the height, with one thing passing, and reflections.
  - Loop: the thing leaves and re-enters off-canvas; the light moves on the water.
  - Pitfall: something too small to read.
- hero · Hero Object: one large object tied to the icon, at low contrast behind or beside the name.
  - Loop: a soft light sweeps across it; rotation of at most 5°.
  - Pitfall: the object competing with the name.

  - Loop: the shapes drift and overlap.
  - Pitfall: looking like wallpaper.
