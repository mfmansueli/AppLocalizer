WHAT GREAT LOOKS LIKE (three real App Store headers, described)
1. A hiking app: a large letter-spaced name with a small pine emblem, centered over layered forest hills in the greens of its icon. Flat illustration with soft gradients and haze between layers. The layers drift slowly at different speeds.
2. A travel app: the view through an airplane window. The dark rounded frame fills the header; the bold name runs across the middle. Behind the glass the sky turns from day blue to sunset orange, to night, to dawn, and back.
3. A coastal game: a shore with a lighthouse cottage, a passing ship and drifting clouds. The sky cycles from day to a warm sunset, to night with stars and a moon, to a pink dawn.

What they share:
- One place or viewpoint that IS the app's world.
- Only the name (and at most one small emblem) as text; no interface and no devices.
- Colors taken from the icon, so the header and the icon read as one brand.
- 3 to 5 depth layers.
- A slow natural cycle of about 20–30 seconds that returns to its start.
- The name large and centered, readable on a phone.
- Calm enough to sit behind the App Store's Back and Share buttons.
